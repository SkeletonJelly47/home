Drive Ahead! Carcade

Drive Ahead! Carcade is a multiplatform, online multiplayer, casual car battler that is best played with friends!

After two or so years of working on Drive Ahead!, we got an amazing opportunity to make a game for Apple Arcade. Our first true multiplatform title, released in November of 2024 for iOS, macOS and tvOS devices. This was a unique chance to get the technical implementation on a solid foundation after working on Drive Ahead for a good while.

During this project I spearheaded pair programming as a practice into the team, after recruiting my good friend and KUAS alumni Sipi Raussi into the company. We wanted to modernize our way of working, improve our speed of learning, overall quality and reduce defect rates. Shortly after we started pair programming, we also started figuring out what kind of automated testing strategy would work for us. Both of us were moderately familiar with test driven development and wanted to find out how we could take those benefits into a Unity project.

Carcade is by far the project I'm most proud of, and I cant wait to make something even better!

Very grateful to the awesome folks at Dodreams who provided me the space to learn. Keep making awesome stuff ❤️

Drive Ahead!

After graduating from KUAS, I started looking for work and was very quickly picked up by the lovely folks at Dodreams, to work on the f2p title Drive Ahead!.

I was brought on to the Drive Ahead! team to work on new game modes, monetization and economy features. I started on UI programming and gradually started branching to client/server code. Working in a long lived code base had its challenges, but was a great learning experience after almost exclusively working on brand new projects in my education.

Alongside the feature work, I started to learn other facets of our pipelines, namely our LiveOps tools and our TeamCity build pipeline. Getting to work on Drive Ahead was a great way to get my hands dirty with live game development.

Thesis - Replicating Source Engine Air Strafing in Unity

I completed my thesis in November of 2021 and is available on Theseus.fi. The document is in english.

The work goes over the history of air strafing, it's various appearances in games, practical usage, the math of air strafing and a game demo made in the Unity game engine. The thesis is made with LaTeX as I wanted to learn a new language and expand my skillset.

This work now concludes my game programming studies at KUAS and I graduated with a Bachelor's Degree in Business Information Technology.

Internship at CSE Entertainment

I had my mandatory 5 month internship at CSE Entertainment, a Kajaani based company delivering exergaming products and services.

I worked on runBEAT, a Unity game that is deployed all over the world. It's played on a motorless treadmill, and meant to be a more social running experience, since people can run at their own pace while still running side by side.

While working on the project, I got a lot of experience working with an existing product that contained a lot of legacy code. A substantial portion of my time was dedicated to refactoring the code to make it more maintainable.

I also got to teach the team GitFlow version control practices with a one week mini project, with all the team members collaborating rapidly.

Ninja game

Dashbot Ninja (2019) is a fast paced PC game where you dash through the office buildings of evil corporates while slicing through their robotic henchmen.

This project was a bit different as we only had 3 people in the team in total. I worked as a gameplay programmer on this project, and was responsible for all of the player mechanics.

We scoped the game for our smaller than usual team, and still aimed for a commercial, finished project. We succeeded in this and the result is my best game I've done to date. The game launched on Steam and Google Play Store simultaneously.

Local couch multiplayer frogs

Frog Out! (2018) is a wacky local multiplayer party game for up to 4 players. It's time for you and your friends to find out who is the slickest and the craftiest frog in the pond. Channel your inner frog and jump to victory!

This was my first project where I took on the role of a designer. Up until this project I was always a programmer, and wanted to try being a designer, so I did.

Turned out designing wasn't my thing and I still learned a lot through out the project. We submitted the game to Assembly Game Dev competition and won first place! We also placed second in the Hatch Party Game competition. The project was released on Steam
